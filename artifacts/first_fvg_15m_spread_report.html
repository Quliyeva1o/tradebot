How spread was applied. Every trade is charged a fixed 3.0-point NAS100 round-trip spread (this repo's existing cost-stress convention, scripts/robustness_analysis.SPREAD_BY_SYMBOL), converted to R via spread / risk_distance and subtracted before P&L. The raw feed's own per-bar spread column reads exactly 0.0 for every bar before 2024 — that's when the broker started recording it live, not a cost-free market — so it was not used directly; using it as-is would have made 2020–2023 look free to trade.
  • Entry = first touch back into the first 3-candle imbalance found after 09:30 NY; stop = the gap's displacement candle body; target = 2R fixed.
  • "Last N" windows are anchored to the last bar in the data, 2026-08-27 — not to today's date. 5m and 15m each use the broker's own native bars, freshly pulled from MT5 (not resampled from M1), so their last-bar timestamps can differ by a bar or two.
  • At most one trade per day, so single-day win-rates below are trivially 0% or 100%.
